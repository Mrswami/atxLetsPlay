# ATX LET'S PLAY: CHARACTER DECK (V1.0)

This is the **"Soul & Prompt" Manifest**. It tracks every character we develop for the ATX Let's Play ecosystem. Each character is mapped to a specific Austin district and a **Low-Poly Painterly** art blueprint.

---

## 🛡️ TIER 1: THE LEGENDS (AUSTIN ICONS)

### 1. NELSON THE RED (The Country Legend)
- **District**: Pease District (Downtown)
- **Lore Tier**: THE KINGS OF THE COURT.
- **Backstory**: A grizzled Austin music icon whose spirit is as legendary as his pigtail braids. He rules the Pease courts with an iconic acoustic guitar that he holds upside down as a pickleball racket. A single tension-snapped guitar string curls into the air.
- **Mandatory Accessory**: Red-stained wooden guitar (Racket mode).
- **Skill Level**: 4.9 (High Intensity)
- **OpenArt Prompt (Refined)**: 
  > `A faceted low-poly 3D character portrait of an iconic, grizzled Austin country legend with long pigtail braids. He is wielding an iconic acoustic guitar upside down like a heavy pickleball racket; a single guitar string is snapped and curled into the air. Triangulated mesh-topology, sharp geometric edges, matte hand-painted gouache textures. Thick ink-brush outlines, soft cel-shading. Austin-Vibe outdoor lighting. Background: Austin Pease Park live oaks under a vibrant sunset. Masterpiece quality 3D.`

## 🎯 TIER 2: DISTRICT ACTIVE (PLAYERS & COACHES)

### 2. MUELLER SYSTEMS-MAVERICK (The Strategist)
- **District**: Mueller (North-East)
- **Lore Tier**: THE DISCIPLINED LOYALISTS.
- **Backstory**: A Mueller-based data scientist who views the court as a live algorithm. Undefeated when the weather is under 80 degrees. He wears a sensor-integrated knit sweatband that tracks his internal "Hype" levels.
- **Mandatory Accessory**: Thick knit athletic sweatband with neon LED status lights.
- **Skill Level**: 3.8 (Medium Intensity)
- **OpenArt Prompt**: 
  > `A triangulated low-poly 3D character portrait of a high-tech athletic player. Highly detailed knit sweatband with LED status highlights. Sharp geometric facets, matte hand-painted textures, thin ink outlines, cel-shaded. Background: A stylized Mueller Lake Park sunrise. 64-bit geometry but modern cinematic lighting. 4k Resolution. Hand-brushed gouache finish.`

### 3. SOCO NEON-SPIKER (The Socialite)
- **District**: South Congress (SoCo / Little Stacy)
- **Lore Tier**: THE VIBRANT CONNECTORS.
- **Backstory**: A social-media influencer from SoCo who values the "vibe" frequency more than the scoreboard. She carries a high-detail neon-etched pickleball racket and wears retro-neon athletic gear.
- **Mandatory Accessory**: High-detail neon-etched graphite racket.
- **Skill Level**: 2.5 (Social-Vibe Peak)
- **OpenArt Prompt**: 
  > `A vibrant low-poly 3D character portrait of a high-energy social sports player. Carrying a high-detail neon-etched pickleball racket. Faceted polygons, matte hand-painted textures, thin ink outlines, high-contrast cel-shading. Background: A stylized South Congress neon-glow sunset. Austin-active aesthetic. Masterpiece low-poly quality. 8k.`

### 4. ZILKER ZEN-SERVER (The Balanced Athlete)
- **District**: Downtown (Zilker Park Great Lawn)
- **Lore Tier**: THE KINGS OF THE COURT.
- **Backstory**: A zen-like athlete who lives for the sunrise sessions at Zilker. She is known for her "silent serve" and wears a thick athletic headband that she never takes off.
- **Mandatory Accessory**: High-detail knit athletic headband and a minimal silver whistle.
- **Skill Level**: 4.2 (High Intensity)
- **OpenArt Prompt**: 
  > `A geometric low-poly 3D character portrait of a calm athletic woman wearing a thick knit athletic headband. Sharp-edged triangulated mesh, soft gouache hand-painted textures, soft cel-shading with ink outlines. High-performance Zilker Park afternoon sunlight focus. Austin-inspired natural park background. Masterpiece quality low-poly art.`

### 5. SHIPE HISTORIAN (The Technical Coach)
- **District**: Hyde Park (Shipe Park)

- **Backstory**: A traditionalists who believes sports are about legacy and form. He only plays at Shipe Park and carries a no-nonsense leather-grip racket.
- **Mandatory Accessory**: Classic leather-grip paddle and a technical whistle on a paracord.
- **Skill Level**: 4.5 (Technical-Purity Peak)
- **OpenArt Prompt**: 
  > `A triangulated low-poly 3D character portrait of a technical sports coach. Holding a high-detail classic leather-grip racket and a silver whistle. Sharp geometric facets, hand-brushed painterly textures, matte 3D finish, inked outlines. High-contrast cel-shading. Background: Historic Hyde Park Austin greens. Masterpiece low-poly quality.`
