# ORACLE PROFILE: AZALEA OF THE UNDERPASS

- **District**: North Austin (The Stitching Zones)
- **Role**: Tier 1 - Urban Oracle
- **Lore Tier**: THE MURAL-SHIFTERS.
- **Backstory**: Azalea is the "Soul of the Hidden Austin." She is the manifestation of the city's street art legacy, living in the quiet, industrial spaces where the railroad tracks meet the dreamscape. She is the guardian of the "Stitching Logic"—the literal visual bridge that binds the surreal map sections together.
- **Soulful DNA**: Contemplative, Dreamy, Urban, Resilient.
- **Mandatory Accessory**: A glowing spray-can of "Liquid Surrealism" and a translucent neon-mesh visor.
