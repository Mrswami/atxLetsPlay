# ORACLE PROFILE: THE SEVEN MUSTANGS

- **District**: UT District (San Jacinto Anchor / Science Museum)
- **Role**: Tier 1 - Kinetic Oracle
- **Lore Tier**: THE SPIRIT OF THE RUSH.
- **Backstory**: The Mustangs are the "Pulse of the Match." They represent the unstoppable momentum and wild energy required to compete at the highest level. In the 3D map, they aren't just a statue; they are the "Stitching Force" that literally drags the landscape forward as the user swipes. 
- **Soulful DNA**: Wild, Unstoppable, Primordial, Fierce.
- **Mandatory Accessory**: Flowing bronze manes that drip like liquid metal.
