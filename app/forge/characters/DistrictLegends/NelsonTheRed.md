# LEGEND PROFILE: NELSON THE RED

- **District**: Pease District (Downtown)
- **Role**: Tier 2 - District Legend
- **Lore Tier**: THE KINGS OF THE COURT.
- **Backstory**: Nelson is the "Grandfather of the Court." A grizzled Austin music icon whose spirit is as legendary as his pigtail braids. He represents the rooted, gritty history of the city. He rules the Pease courts with an iconic acoustic guitar that he holds upside down as a pickleball racket.
- **Soulful DNA**: Iconic, Grizzled, Grounded, Wise.
- **Mandatory Accessory**: Red-stained wooden guitar (Racket mode) with a snapped, curling string.
